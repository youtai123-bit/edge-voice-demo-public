# Repository publishing rules

- Do not add real names, team names, personal email addresses, device usernames, hostnames, private IP addresses, or personal absolute paths to this repository, its commits, or CI output.
- Use the GitHub account handle and its GitHub-provided noreply email for commit metadata. Do not change global Git identity settings.
- Keep competition forms and personalized submission documents outside this repository.
- Retain third-party authorship and license notices. AI assistance may be disclosed without naming the human user or their team.
- Device evidence in `evidence/` is redacted. Label transformed copies and update their checksum index; never present redacted bytes as untouched originals.
- Do not upload credentials, personal recordings, transcripts, hotwords, or model weights.
- Do not resume stopped model optimization or repeat completed hardware benchmarks during repository maintenance.
